        This page is a demo built from a written description of a business. The business, the prices and the slots on it are made up, and the phone number is in the 555-01xx range that exists for fiction. No reviews, ratings or customer names appear anywhere on it, because a new page has not earned any.

        To make it real, a person wires the outbox to something that sends — and keeps the approval step in front of it. Every row in the outbox carries the request id, the revision and the approval it came from, so the thing that sends can check them again on its side.
