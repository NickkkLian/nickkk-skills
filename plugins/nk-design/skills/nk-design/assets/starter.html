    About this demo

    Every number on this page is computed in your browser from synthetic rows generated from seed 42. Nothing is loaded from a server or sent anywhere, and your decisions change this tab only. The search, status filter, sort and open row are kept in the address, so a copied link opens the same view. Add ?seed=7&rows=300 to the address to generate another batch (up to 2,000 rows).

    Not verified here

    Real bank or workbook exports (every row here is generated), screen readers, browsers other than Chromium, touch devices, and right-to-left text.
