the hard part of walkposter was one number: the climb.
gps height jumps by a few metres while you stand still.
a flat 3 km walk by the river came out as 240 m of climb.
=====
what fixed it: ignore every change smaller than 3 m, then add up what is left.
the same river walk now reads 18 m.
my legs agree with the second number.
=====
the 3 m is one setting you can change.
