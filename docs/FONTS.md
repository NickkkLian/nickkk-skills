# Self-hosted fonts

- Bricolage Grotesque, regular and bold, from the supplied licensed font collection. SIL OFL 1.1, licence `fonts/BricolageGrotesque-OFL.txt`. Converted to WOFF2 with fontTools, outlines unchanged.
- Red Hat Mono, regular and bold, from the supplied licensed font collection. SIL OFL 1.1, licence `fonts/RedHatMono-OFL.txt`. Converted to WOFF2 with fontTools, outlines unchanged.

No network downloads. Two static weights or a variable weight axis are included.
