nickkk-skills

Privacy policy

Applies to the nk-* agent skills published by Nick Lian at github.com/NickkkLian, including their listings in plugin directories. Last updated 2026-10-01.

The short version

These skills run where your assistant runs them. They send nothing to me. I do not collect, receive, store or sell any data about you or your files.

What a skill is

A skill is a folder of written instructions and small Python scripts. Your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Codex or Claude Code) reads the instructions, and the scripts run on your computer or in the assistant's own sandbox. There is no server of mine behind them, no account and no sign-up.

What I collect

Personal data collected by meNone.
Purposes of useNone, because nothing is collected.
RecipientsNone. Nothing is shared or sold.
RetentionNothing reaches me, so nothing is kept.
Analytics or tracking in the skillsNone. I cannot see who installed a skill or what you do with it.

The scripts in the skills make no network requests of their own. Your files, prompts and results stay where your assistant works with them.

Other parties you should know about

  • Your assistant. The company that runs your assistant (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) handles your conversation and the files you give it under its own privacy policy. The skills do not change that.
  • Programs you already use. Some skills start programs that are on your machine, such as git, a browser for a print check, or your assistant's command-line tool. Those programs talk to the services you have set up for them (for example your own git remote), as they would if you ran them yourself.
  • Web fonts in generated pages. Several skills write an HTML page for you (a report, a deck, a tool). When you open such a page with an internet connection, your browser may load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives an ordinary font request (your IP address and browser details). Your data is inside the page file and is not sent. Offline, the pages fall back to system fonts.
  • This website and the code. This site and the repositories are hosted by GitHub, which keeps its own visit logs under GitHub's privacy statement. I set no cookies and run no analytics here. The site remembers your colour theme in your browser's local storage.

Your controls

Uninstall a skill at any time from your assistant's plugin settings. Files a skill wrote are ordinary files in your own folders: delete them as you would any file. There is nothing to request from me, because I hold nothing.

Children

The skills are developer and office tools and are not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the earlier text stays in the repository history. Questions: open an issue at github.com/NickkkLian/nickkk-skills/issues.
