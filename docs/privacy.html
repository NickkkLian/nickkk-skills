Effective date: October 1, 2026 · Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nick Lian ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with (a) the nk-* agent skills and plugins published at github.com/NickkkLian, including their listings in third-party plugin directories (the "Skills"), and (b) the website at nickkklian.github.io/nickkk-skills (the "Site"). By installing or using the Skills, or by visiting the Site, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

1. Summary

The Skills are executed entirely within the environment of the AI assistant you use. We do not operate any server that receives data from the Skills, and we do not collect, store, sell or share personal information through them. The Site does not use cookies, advertising or analytics.

2. How the Skills operate

Each Skill consists of written instructions and Python scripts that are distributed as files. When a Skill is used, your AI assistant (for example ChatGPT, Codex or Claude Code) reads the instructions and runs the scripts on your device or within the assistant's own sandboxed environment. The Skills require no account with us and no registration. The scripts included in the Skills do not initiate network requests. Files, prompts and outputs processed by a Skill remain within your environment and that of your assistant.

4. How we use and disclose information

Because we do not collect personal information through the Skills or the Site, we do not use, sell, rent, share or otherwise disclose personal information, and we do not use any information for advertising, profiling or the training of machine-learning models.

5. Third-party services

Your use of the Skills and the Site may involve third parties whose processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, which we encourage you to review:

  • AI assistant providers. The provider of the assistant in which you run a Skill (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) processes your conversations and files in accordance with its own policies. The Skills do not alter that processing.
  • Software on your device. Certain Skills invoke software already installed in your environment, such as Git, a web browser or your assistant's command-line interface. Such software communicates only with the services you have configured for it, as it would if you ran it directly.
  • Google Fonts. Some Skills generate HTML documents (for example reports or slide decks). When such a document is opened with an internet connection, your browser may request font files from Google Fonts, which results in Google receiving standard request information such as your IP address and browser type. The contents of the document are not transmitted. Without a connection, system fonts are used.
  • GitHub. The Site and the Skills' source repositories are hosted by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log technical information about visits, including IP addresses, in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Data retention

We do not receive personal information through the Skills or the Site and therefore retain none. Files created by a Skill are stored in locations you control and remain there until you delete them.

7. Security

The Skills are published as open source, and their complete contents can be inspected before installation. Because no data is transmitted to us, the security of information processed by a Skill depends on your own environment and on your assistant provider.

8. Your rights and choices

You may uninstall a Skill at any time through your assistant's plugin or skill settings, and you may delete any file a Skill has created. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable law to access, correct or delete personal information held about you. As we hold no personal information relating to users of the Skills or visitors to the Site, there is ordinarily nothing for us to provide, correct or delete; you may nonetheless contact us with any request or question using the details in Section 11. Requests concerning data held by a third party listed in Section 5 should be directed to that party.

9. Children's privacy

The Skills and the Site are intended for a general professional audience and are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above indicates when it was most recently revised, and prior versions remain available in the public history of the repository. Material changes take effect when the revised Policy is posted on this page.
