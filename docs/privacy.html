1. Applicability

This Policy applies to Personal Data that we process in connection with the Services. It does not apply to:

  • the products and services of third parties through which the Skills are distributed or executed, including AI assistant providers and plugin directory operators, whose processing of your Personal Data is governed by their own privacy policies;
  • third-party software that a Skill may invoke within your environment; or
  • files, prompts and other content that you process using the Skills, which remain under your control and are not transmitted to us.

2. Overview of the Services

The Skills are designed to operate locally. Each Skill consists of written instructions and Python scripts distributed as files. When you use a Skill, your AI assistant (for example ChatGPT, Codex or Claude Code) reads those instructions and executes the scripts on your device or within the assistant's own execution environment. The Skills do not require an account with us, do not communicate with any server operated by us, and their scripts do not initiate network requests.

3. Personal Data We Collect

3.1 Personal Data You Provide

We do not require you to provide Personal Data in order to install or use the Skills or to visit the Site. If you choose to contact us, for example by opening an issue in one of our public repositories, we receive the information you elect to provide, which may include your account username, your contact details and the contents of your communication.

3.2 Personal Data Collected Automatically

We do not collect Personal Data automatically through the Skills. The Skills contain no analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising technologies, and we have no technical means of identifying who has installed a Skill or how it is used.

We do not collect Personal Data automatically through the Site. The Site is served by a third-party hosting provider, which may record technical information such as IP addresses in its own server logs, as described in Section 6.

3.3 Personal Data from Third Parties

We do not obtain Personal Data about you from third parties. Operators of plugin directories may make aggregated, non-identifying statistics available to us, such as the number of installations of a Skill.

3.4 Content You Process Using the Skills

Files, prompts and outputs that you process using the Skills are handled within your own environment and that of your AI assistant provider. Such content is not transmitted to, accessed by or stored by us.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We use the limited Personal Data described in Section 3.1 solely to:

  • respond to your inquiries, requests and reports;
  • maintain, correct and improve the Skills in response to issues you report; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

We do not use Personal Data for advertising, marketing or profiling, and we do not use Personal Data, or any content processed using the Skills, to train machine-learning models.

5. How We Disclose Personal Data

We do not sell Personal Data, and we do not share Personal Data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose Personal Data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Public communications. Information that you submit through public channels, such as an issue in a public repository, is visible to the public by the nature of that channel.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose Personal Data where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation or valid legal process.
  • Protection of rights. We may disclose Personal Data where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to protect the rights, property or safety of any person.

6. Third-Party Services

Your use of the Services may involve the following third parties, each of which processes information in accordance with its own terms and privacy policy. We encourage you to review those documents.

  • AI assistant providers. The provider of the assistant in which you run a Skill (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) processes your conversations and files under its own policies. The Skills do not alter that processing.
  • Software in your environment. Certain Skills invoke software already installed in your environment, such as Git, a web browser or your assistant's command-line interface. That software communicates only with the services you have configured for it.
  • Google Fonts. Certain Skills generate HTML documents. When such a document is opened while connected to the internet, your browser may request font files from Google Fonts, in which case Google receives standard request information, including your IP address and browser type. The contents of the document are not transmitted.
  • GitHub. The Site and the source repositories for the Skills are hosted by GitHub, Inc., which may log technical information relating to your visit in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the laws of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland apply, we process the Personal Data described in Section 3.1 on the following legal bases: our legitimate interests in responding to communications and maintaining the Skills; compliance with legal obligations to which we are subject; and, where applicable, your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

8. International Data Transfers

Personal Data that you provide when contacting us may be processed in Canada and in other countries in which our service providers operate, including the United States. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from the laws of your jurisdiction. Where required by applicable law, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data contained in communications with us only for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve the matter concerned, and thereafter as required to comply with legal obligations or to resolve disputes. Communications submitted through public channels remain available on the relevant third-party platform until removed by you, by us or by that platform. We do not retain content processed using the Skills, as it is never transmitted to us.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the limited Personal Data we hold against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. The Skills are published as open source, and their complete contents may be inspected prior to installation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request access to, and a copy of, the Personal Data we hold about you;
  • request the correction or deletion of that Personal Data;
  • object to, or request the restriction of, our processing of that Personal Data;
  • withdraw your consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 16. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. You may also uninstall any Skill at any time through your assistant's settings and delete any file a Skill has created within your environment.

12. Jurisdiction-Specific Information

12.1 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

In addition to the rights described in Section 11, you have the right to data portability where applicable and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

12.2 United States

For residents of California and other states with comprehensive privacy laws: in the preceding twelve months, the only category of personal information we have collected is identifiers and the contents of communications that individuals have voluntarily submitted to us, collected for the purposes described in Section 4. We do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than those permitted by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

12.3 Canada

You have the right to request access to and correction of your Personal Data in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy legislation, and to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the relevant provincial authority.

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site does not use cookies, advertising technologies or analytics. The Site stores a display-theme preference in your browser's local storage; that preference remains on your device, is not transmitted to us and may be cleared through your browser settings. The Skills do not use cookies or similar technologies.

14. Children

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 13, or such higher minimum age as applies in your jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children. If you believe that a child has provided Personal Data to us, please contact us so that we may delete it.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The "Last Updated" date above indicates when this Policy was most recently revised. Where changes are material, we will provide notice by updating this page before the changes take effect. Prior versions remain available in the public revision history of the repository.
