What this report does not answer

Limits of this data

  • One shop and one year, with no weather or holiday data.
  • Each weekday average in the last quarter rests on about thirteen days of that weekday, a small sample.
  • Sales with no amount are left out of every sum; each figure says how many it skipped.
  • Rows exported twice are counted as they are; the profile lists them.
